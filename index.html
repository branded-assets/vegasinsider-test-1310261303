Key findings

Six quick takeaways from the coaches fans were most and least likely to pick first.

01
Overall leader

Brian Keefe and the Wizards set the pace

7.87%

Brian Keefe of the Washington Wizards drew the highest overall share at 7.87%, finishing 1.39 percentage points ahead of JJ Redick.

02
Second and third place

JJ Redick held second, with Nick Nurse third

6.48%

JJ Redick ranked second overall at 6.48%, while Nick Nurse followed in third at 4.89%. That kept the runner-up gap over third place at 1.59 percentage points.

03
Fanbase perspective

Miami fans were far more likely to pick Erik Spoelstra

17.14%

Among Heat fans, 17.14% selected Erik Spoelstra, compared with 2.34% of fans overall. That is a 14.80-percentage-point difference, describing survey responses rather than actual firing odds.

04
Age split

Joe Mazzulla topped both younger age groups

8.74%

Joe Mazzulla led among fans ages 18–24 with 8.74% and ages 25–34 with 7.89%. Brian Keefe led each age group from 35 upward.

05
Eastern Conference

Brian Keefe was the highest-picked Eastern Conference coach

7.87%

Among coaches from Eastern Conference teams, Brian Keefe ranked first at 7.87%, ahead of Nick Nurse at 4.89% and Tiago Splitter at 4.82%.

06
Western Conference

JJ Redick was the top Western Conference coach

6.48%

Among coaches from Western Conference teams, JJ Redick led at 6.48%, ahead of Dusty May at 4.52% and Will Hardy at 4.46%.

Survey methodology and limitations

Question 5 of a commissioned U.S. NBA fan survey, conducted October 2–6, 2026.

Survey design and question

A commissioned online survey gathered 4,398 responses from U.S. NBA fans. The question analyzed here was: “Which NBA Head Coach do you think will be fired first?” The results contain 30 named coach-and-team response options, retained as listed in the survey.

What the percentages mean

Each overall percentage represents the share of all surveyed fans selecting that coach. For gender, age, relationship status, NBA fanbase, region and state, percentages use the named subgroup's own respondent base. These are fan perceptions recorded before the 2026–27 NBA season, not forecasts of real coaching departures or probabilities of dismissal.

Rankings and rounding

Coaches are ranked by their underlying response shares, with ties sharing a competition rank. Percentages are shown to two decimal places, so displayed values may not add to exactly 100.00%. Percentage-point differences are calculated from the underlying shares.

Subgroup and state sampling cautions

Gender, age, relationship, NBA fanbase, regional and all 50 state breakdowns are provided. Subgroup sizes vary, sometimes substantially, so differences should be interpreted descriptively. State results describe this survey's respondents and are not representative state polling estimates.

Statistical and sourcing limits

The supplied survey summary does not specify recruitment methods, sampling frame, response rate, quotas, statistical weighting or a margin of error. No significance testing was provided. The individual respondent-level dataset was not supplied, only aggregate Q5 tabulations. Differences across coaches or groups should not be presented as statistically significant or causal.

Source and timing

Source: Commissioned U.S. NBA fan survey, October 2–6, 2026; Question 5 tabulations. Coach–team pairings are from the October 2026 survey response options and are not a live coaching roster.
